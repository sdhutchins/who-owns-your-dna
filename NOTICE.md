# Data provenance and third-party notice

Who Owns Your DNA? combines project-authored policy records with an archived
snapshot of the National Human Genome Research Institute Genome Statute and
Legislation Database.

## NHGRI source archive

Archived records retain their original NHGRI wording and source details.
NHGRI states that information on its website is generally public domain unless
otherwise indicated and requests acknowledgment. Public pages identify the
archive as “Courtesy: National Human Genome Research Institute.”

- Source: <https://www.genome.gov/about-genomics/policy-issues/Genome-Statute-Legislation-Database>
- Copyright policy: <https://www.genome.gov/about-nhgri/Policies-Guidance/Copyright>
- Local provenance documentation: [`docs/nhgri-source-import.md`](docs/nhgri-source-import.md)

## Primary and third-party sources

The dataset links to statutes, bills, regulations, court materials, agency
